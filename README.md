## About

Log each service with the date and mileage, and it stays saved between visits. Useful for remembering when you last changed the oil, rotated the tires, or dealt with anything else without digging through receipts.

Everything is stored locally in your browser with `localStorage`, so there's no account and nothing leaves your machine.

---

## Features

- Add entries with service name, date, and mileage
- Entries saved automatically to `localStorage`
- Delete individual entries
- Survives a page refresh

---

## Usage

1. Type the service (e.g. Oil Change), pick a date, and enter the mileage.
2. Click **Add Entry**.
3. Delete any entry with its Delete button.

Try it live at [YOUR_LIVE_URL](https://YOUR_LIVE_URL/), or clone the repo and open `index.html` in your browser.
